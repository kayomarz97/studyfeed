## Plain-language (what this builds)

A **topic dossier** is a pre-built "read up on X" page. For every meaningful entity (a disease, a
drug, an electrolyte problem) we gather **every grounded fact we already extracted about it, from
every book**, group those facts by medical subject, and stitch them into one long scrollable page —
plus the list of questions that touch it and its connections in the concept map. It's assembled
**deterministically from the atoms** (no new AI writing, so nothing can be hallucinated and a rebuild
gives byte-identical pages), and it inherits the importance of its most important fact.

## Technical

**Inputs (all already on disk):**
- `concepts.jsonl` — canonical entities (id, name, canonical_key, aliases, centrality, guideline_sensitive).
- `graph_edges_raw.jsonl` — `{s,r,o,source_id,rationale}`; `rationale` = the atom's `statement[:240]`.
  This is the **atom↔concept link** (same one `importance.py` uses): `alias.key(s|o)` → `canonical_key` → concept id.
- `atoms_merged.jsonl` — the deduped atoms (statement, detail, source_id, page_ref, citations[]).
- `atom_importance.jsonl` — per-atom 1–5 (keyed by `stable_ref` = statement[:60]).
- `concept_edges.jsonl` — typed concept→concept edges (for the connections list).
- `mcqs.jsonl` + `mcq_importance.jsonl` — for related-question links (phrase-match concept name/aliases in stems).
- `classification.jsonl` — source_id → subject (**only textbook/pearls carry a subject**; past-paper/qbank are subject-less).

**Scope (measured, profile-driven — no magic numbers in code):** 37,795 concepts all have ≥1 atom, but a
1-atom concept is an atom, not a dossier. Build a page when the concept clears a floor:
`≥ dossier.min_atoms (3)` constituent atoms **OR** spans `≥ dossier.min_subjects (2)` subjects.
Measured counts: **8,194 dossiers** (7,904 with ≥3 atoms ∪ 3,237 cross-subject). Thresholds live in
`profiles/mrcp_part1.yaml` under a new `dossier:` block; the excluded count is **logged** (no silent cap).

**Assembly (deterministic):**
1. Build `stmt[:240] → {concept ids}` from raw edges; invert to `concept id → [constituent atoms]`
   (distinct atoms; an atom that mentions the concept in any of its triples counts once).
2. Subject sectioning: each atom's subject via `source_id → classification.subject`. Sections ordered by
   blueprint weight desc then name; subject-less atoms (past-paper/qbank) go to a final
   **"Question-bank & exam notes"** section. Atoms within a section ordered by importance desc, then statement (stable).
3. `body_md`: `# Title` + one-line stat line + `## Subject` sections (each atom = a bullet with its
   statement, detail, and `[work p.page]` citation) + `## Concept connections` (from `concept_edges`, top by
   distinct_source_count, capped + logged) . **Every line cites a page → grounded.**
4. `related_mcq_idxs`: MCQs whose stem+explanation contains the concept name or an alias (phrase match,
   name length ≥ 4 to avoid junk); capped to top-K by MCQ importance (logged). Stored as mcqs.jsonl idx
   (packaging resolves to mcq ids).
5. `importance` = **max** of constituent atoms' importance (§5d); `importance_score` = that atom's score;
   `importance_signals_json` = `{rule:"max_of_atoms", from_atom, signals}`.
6. `stable_key` = `sha256(("topic|" + canonical_key).encode())[:16]` — 1:1 with the canonical concept,
   survives rebuild, namespaced away from atom/mcq keys (documented for pack.py at C13).

**Deferred fields (assets don't exist until C11):** `figure_ids_json`/`related_flashcard_ids_json` = `[]`
now; backfilled at C11/C13. Noted in output + journal.
